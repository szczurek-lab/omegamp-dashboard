## Murine Infection Models -- In Vivo Efficacy

Selected leads were advanced from in vitro characterization to two mouse models of *Acinetobacter baumannii* ATCC 19606 infection: a topical **skin scarification** model and a systemic **neutropenic thigh** model. Both quantify bacterial burden as CFU recovered from the infected tissue, alongside body weight as a tolerability readout.

### Skin scarification model

Six-week-old female CD-1 mice were anesthetized, and the dorsal skin was shaved and superficially abraded to create a linear wound. *A. baumannii* ATCC 19606 (6.66 x 10^5 CFU/mL) was applied directly to the scarified area. Peptides were administered topically to the wound at 10x MIC, 1 h post-infection. Bacterial burden in the excised wound was quantified at days 2 and 4. Polymyxin B, a last-resort antibiotic for *A. baumannii*, serves as the benchmark control.

Six leads spanning the generation modes were tested: de novo (Ω-DP-52, Ω-DP-19), inactive-to-active BoCo1 analogs (Ω-AT-BoCo1-5, Ω-AT-BoCo1-9), and motif-guided cecropin analogs (Ω-AMT-cecropin-1, Ω-AMT-cecropin-4).

### Neutropenic thigh model

Mice were rendered neutropenic with cyclophosphamide (150 and 100 mg/kg intraperitoneally, on days 0 and 3 before infection). *A. baumannii* ATCC 19606 (9.66 x 10^5 CFU/mL) was established by intramuscular injection into the right thigh. Peptides were administered **intraperitoneally** (systemic exposure) at 10x MIC, 2 h post-infection, and burden was quantified at days 6 and 8. Levofloxacin serves as the antibiotic control.

Only proteolytically stable leads were advanced here: Ω-MT-bZIP-8 and Ω-AMT-pa4-1, the two peptides that retained the most intact material over 6 h of protease exposure (see Proteolytic stability, below).

### Reading the plots

- **Bacterial burden** (box plots): each box is the interquartile range with the median line; whiskers span the range; points are individual mice (n = 6 per group per timepoint). Lower is better -- a downward shift versus the untreated Control is a reduction in log10 CFU/g.
- **Body-weight change** (line plots): mean +/- SEM relative to day 0, with a dashed line at 100% (baseline). Weight maintained at or above baseline indicates the treatment was well tolerated.

Significance in the manuscript figure is from Dunn's test versus Control following a Kruskal-Wallis test (skin), and additionally versus levofloxacin (thigh); the dashboard shows the underlying per-mouse distributions.

### Proteolytic stability

Because serum proteases degrade peptides before they reach a systemic site, proteolytic stability was used as a filter ahead of the thigh model. Five analog and motif-guided leads were incubated with protease and the fraction of intact peptide was tracked over 6 h. Most fell below 10% within 1 h, whereas Ω-AMT-pa4-1 and Ω-MT-bZIP-8 retained roughly 63% and 40% at 6 h -- and were the two carried into the systemic model.
